4. Clinical Perspective: Proactive Care vs. Avoiding Over-Intervention

✅ Why These Findings Are Useful
  • Proactive Medication Safety: Documenting pharmacogenomic diplotypes prevents avoidable drug side effects and subtherapeutic dosing before a prescription is started.
  • Routine Preventive Care: High-level wellness topics integrate naturally into the annual primary care physical.
  • Specialist Coordination: Sharing finding-specific precautions supports informed care without unnecessary medicalization.
🛑 Why Aggressive Intervention Is Not Needed
  • Single-Copy Carrier Reassurance: Single-copy recessive carriers retain a functional gene copy and maintain normal baseline health.
  • Absence of Biallelic Recessive Disease: Zero two-copy pathogenic recessive disease genotypes are present in this profile.
  • Pathway Modifiers Are Not Diagnoses: Common physiological modifiers should never trigger unprovoked medication changes or invasive testing.
