POLG p.Gly737Arg · c.2209G>C · rs121918054 Heterozygous Pathogenic AVI 30.3 / CADD 26.3 / REV 0.936
Coding missense variant (p.Gly737Arg) in POLG (Spinocerebellar ataxia with epilepsy (OMIM #203700)). Mitochondrial DNA polymerase is heterotrimeric, consisting of a homodimer of accessory subunits plus a catalytic subunit. Clinical Impact: Heterozygous carriers are typically asymptomatic at baseline, but carry a strict FDA Boxed Warning contraindication against valproic acid / divalproex sodium due to risk of fatal valproate-induced hepatic necrosis; verify baseline AST/ALT on annual metabolic panels.
TAT p.Arg57Ter · c.169C>T · rs118203914 Heterozygous Pathogenic AVI 38.8 / CADD 36.0
Premature stop-gained variant (p.Arg57Ter) in TAT, associated with autosomal recessive Tyrosinemia type II (OMIM #276600). This nuclear gene encodes a mitochondrial protein tyrosine aminotransferase which is present in the liver and catalyzes the conversion of L-tyrosine into p-hydroxyphenylpyruvate. Clinical Impact: Heterozygous carriers retain ~50% hepatic enzymatic activity via the wild-type allele, remain clinically asymptomatic on an unrestricted diet, and require no dietary tyrosine/phenylalanine restriction (optional one-time fasting plasma tyrosine < 100 µmol/L confirms normal clearance).
ATM c.1236-2del · rs1565381646 Heterozygous Pathogenic Analytical VCF
Canonical splice-site variant (c.1236-2del) in ATM (Hereditary cancer-predisposing syndrome). The protein encoded by this gene belongs to the PI3/PI4-kinase family. This protein is an important cell cycle checkpoint kinase that phosphorylates; thus, it functions as a regulator of a wide variety of downstream p… Clinical Impact: Heterozygous carriers have moderately elevated breast and pancreatic cancer susceptibility; NCCN guidelines recommend annual breast MRI and mammography, family history review for pancreatic screening, and minimizing unnecessary diagnostic ionizing radiation.
VDR c.-1172A>G · rs4516035 Heterozygous Pathogenic AVI 2.1 / CADD 5.4
Canonical splice-site variant (c.-1172A>G) in VDR (Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease). This gene encodes vitamin D3 receptor, which is a member of the nuclear hormone receptor superfamily of ligand-inducible transcription factors. Clinical Impact: Modulates baseline calcitriol receptor transcriptional activity; maintain serum 25(OH)D at 35–55 ng/mL via routine cholecalciferol (Vitamin D3) supplementation.
MC1R p.Asp294His · c.880G>C · rs1805009 Heterozygous Risk Factor AVI 24.5 / CADD 26.9 / REV 0.608
Coding missense variant (p.Asp294His) in MC1R (Skin and Hair Hypopigmentation (OMIM #203200)). This intronless gene encodes the receptor protein for melanocyte-stimulating hormone (MSH). The encoded protein, a seven pass transmembrane G protein coupled receptor, controls melanogenesis. Clinical Impact: Confers elevated cutaneous UV sensitivity and melanoma/non-melanoma skin cancer susceptibility; manage via annual full-body dermatologic skin exams and broad-spectrum SPF 30+ photoprotection.
AMPD1 p.Glu435Gly · c.1304A>G · rs764950035 Heterozygous VUS AVI 28.2 / CADD 32.0 / REV 0.949
Coding missense variant (p.Glu435Gly) in AMPD1 (Muscle AMP deaminase deficiency). Adenosine monophosphate deaminase 1 catalyzes the deamination of AMP to IMP in skeletal muscle and plays an important role in the purine nucleotide cycle. Clinical Impact: Single-allele heterozygous carriers are generally asymptomatic; check serum Creatine Kinase (CK) if exercise-induced cramping occurs or prior to escalating high-dose statin therapy.
CTH p.Thr67Ile · c.200C>T · rs28941785 Heterozygous Modifier AVI 27.8 / CADD 25.5 / REV 0.931
Coding missense variant (p.Thr67Ile) in CTH (CTH-related disorder (OMIM #219500)). This gene encodes a cytoplasmic enzyme in the trans-sulfuration pathway that converts cystathione derived from methionine into cysteine. Clinical Impact: Single-allele heterozygous variants retain wild-type enzymatic reserve; verify normal fasting plasma homocysteine (< 12 µmol/L) and adequate Vitamin B6/folate/B12 cofactor status.

4. Clinical Decision Calculus: Balanced Interventional Rationale

✅ Arguments FOR Targeted Preventive Surveillance & PGx
  • Immediate Medication Safety: Documenting CPIC pharmacogenomic diplotypes prevents avoidable drug toxicity and therapeutic failure before the first prescription is filled.
  • Low-Cost Biomarker Baselines: Pathway-matched venous blood tests incorporate seamlessly into the annual physical without procedural diagnostic burdens.
  • Proactive Specialist Coordination: Sharing variant-specific guardrails ensures appropriate situational precautions without over-medicalization.
🛑 Arguments AGAINST Aggressive Over-Intervention
  • Heterozygous Carrier & Modifier Reserve: Heterozygous variants retain an intact wild-type parental allele (~50–100% capacity) and do not warrant invasive diagnostic procedures.
  • Phased Allelic Reassurance: Whole-genome parental phasing confirms 0 confirmed pathogenic biallelic (trans) recessive compound-heterozygotes.
  • Polygenic Modifiers ≠ Deterministic Disease: Modest statistical variants must never trigger unprovoked chronic anticoagulation or off-label polypharmacy.
