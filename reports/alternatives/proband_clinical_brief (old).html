Canonical splice-site variant (c.79+1G>A) in CBLIF (autosomal recessive Intrinsic Factor Deficiency / Cobalamin Malabsorption (OMIM #261000)). This gene is a member of the cobalamin transport protein family. It encodes a glycoprotein secreted by parietal cells of the gastric mucosa and is required for adequate absorption of vitamin B12. Clinical Impact: Heterozygous carriers preserve intrinsic factor secretion from the wild-type allele; periodic serum Vitamin B12 (> 350 pg/mL) and methylmalonic acid (MMA) verify cellular cobalamin stores.

This gene encodes a member of the gap junction protein family (gap junction beta 2) but is more commonly known as connexin 26. Clinical Impact: Heterozygous carriers typically maintain normal adult hearing thresholds; establish a baseline pure-tone audiogram and exercise caution with ototoxic aminoglycoside antibiotics when alternatives exist.

🛑 Arguments AGAINST Aggressive Over-Intervention

  • Phased Allelic Reassurance: Whole-genome parental phasing confirms 0 confirmed pathogenic biallelic (trans) recessive compound-heterozygotes.
  • Polygenic Modifiers ≠ Deterministic Disease: Modest statistical variants must never trigger unprovoked chronic anticoagulation or off-label polypharmacy.
