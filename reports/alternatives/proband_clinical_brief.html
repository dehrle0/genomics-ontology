Executive Summary of Key Findings

    • CBLIF (Intrinsic Factor Deficiency / Cobalamin Malabsorption) - heterozygous carrier status

    • PEX6 (Peroxisome biogenesis disorder 4A / Zellweger) - heterozygous carrier status

  • Actionable Medication Safety:

    • Avoid or select alternatives for F5 due to systemic estrogens, combined oral contraceptives, and HRT use.

  • Preventive Care Takeaway:

    • No invasive diagnostic procedures are required for single-copy carrier findings.
    • Focus on high-level wellness and screening topics in Section 2 of the patient's care plan.

CBLIF · Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin) & Folate Absorption: Canonical splice-site variant (c.79+1G>A) in CBLIF (autosomal recessive Intrinsic Factor Deficiency / Cobalamin Malabsorption (OMIM #261000)). This gene is a member of the cobalamin transport protein family. It encodes a glycoprotein secreted by parietal cells of the gastric mucosa and is required for adequate absorption of vitamin B12. Clinical Impact: Heterozygous carriers preserve intrinsic factor production from the functional allele; periodic Vitamin B12 checks during routine physicals help confirm healthy cellular stores.
Single-Copy (Heterozygous) Recessive Carrier: Retains one functional gene copy with normal baseline physiological capacity; does not cause the classic biallelic condition.

Section 5 · Clinical Perspective: Balancing Proactive Care vs. Over-Intervention

✅ Why These Findings Are Useful
  • Proactive Medication Safety: Recording pharmacogenomic metabolizer status in the medical chart helps select effective medications and avoid preventable side effects before a prescription is started.
  • Informed Annual Checkups: Highlighting relevant biological pathways allows the Primary Care Physician to tailor routine wellness checks and age-appropriate preventive screening.
🛑 Why Aggressive Intervention Is Not Needed
  • Single-Copy Carrier Reassurance: No two-copy (biallelic) recessive disease genotypes are present; single-copy recessive carriers retain a functional gene copy and maintain normal baseline health.
  • Modifier Variants Are Not Diagnoses: Common pathway modifiers indicate mild physiological tendencies and should never trigger unnecessary procedures or unprovoked medication changes.
